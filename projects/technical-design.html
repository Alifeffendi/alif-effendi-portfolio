Turning Ideas Into Technical Drawings.

Technical Drawing & Design represents an exploration of computer-aided design and technical drafting in the field of engineering and building design.

The work involves using AutoCAD to create and develop technical drawings, including building layouts, dimensions, and other design representations.

This experience supports the understanding of technical visualization, drawing precision, and the relationship between design concepts and engineering documentation.
